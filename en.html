🗺 Suwon Travel Guide
🏯 History & Culture — Hwaseong Palace & Area ▶

We recommend Suwon Hwaseong Haenggung Palace. Hop on the Hwaseong Eochacha (royal carriage tour) for a relaxed full tour of the fortress.

  • 🎨 Suwon Media Center — Near Haenggung-dong. Free media art exhibition themed around Hwaseong Fortress.
  • 🎈 Flying Suwon — Stunning aerial views of Hwaseong Palace. Sunset and night views are especially recommended 🌇 Check operating status before visiting as it may be closed in bad weather.
🌿 Nature & Relaxation — Irwol Arboretum ▶

Perfect for a leisurely stroll. Grab a coffee at the café inside and enjoy the scenic views at your own pace ☕

🛍 Shopping ▶
  • 🏬 Starfield Suwon — ~15 min walk
  • 🏬 Time Villas Suwon — at Suwon Station
  • 🏬 AK Plaza — at Suwon Station
🍽 Haenggung-dong Restaurants ▶
  • 🍕 Low Five — Try the signature corn pizza (near Sunkyung Library)
  • 🍳 Farm to Hanok (Hanok brunch café) — Chicken egg stew & oven sandwich recommended (near Low Five)
  • 🥗 Daldal-han Bueok — Great value bibimbap, clean and tasty
  • 🍵 Pyeongjidan — Matcha specialist. Both cakes and drinks are excellent
  • 🥟 Yeonmil — Famous for juicy Chinese-style dumplings. Dry tofu dish and crystal dumplings also recommended
🍽 Starfield Restaurants ▶
  • 🥖 Bon Nouvelle (1F) — Excellent bakery. The Sigoklppang & Mochiri series (out around 11am) are a must-try
  • 🍜 Hyotteu (2F) — Delicious Vietnamese pho
  • 🥞 Hatcho (2F) — Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki
  • 🍜 Lee Byeongtae Hamheung Naengmyeon (7F) — Cold noodle + dumpling set recommended. Great for sharing multiple dishes
  • 🥩 Seongsanmyeongga (7F) — Hanwoo eoboktang (beef offal soup) recommended, especially for family dining. Clean and delicious
🍽 Suwon Station Restaurants ▶
  • 🍴 Time Villas 3F Dining Avenue — Many great restaurants gathered in one place
  • 🍣 Barmi Cymbidium — Fresh and affordable all-you-can-eat sushi buffet
